# PSA vs CGC vs Beckett: which grading service wins in 2026

Grading a card can triple its value. But it costs time and money, and not all grading companies are equal. Especially if you are in Europe.

We graded 200 cards over the last 18 months with all three major services. Here are the concrete numbers.

## How much it really costs in Europe

**PSA (Value Tier)**

- Nominal US price: $19 (about €17.50)
- US round-trip shipping: €80-120 (for 20-card package)
- Time: 60-90 days
- European hub: Tokyo. Cheaper shipping from Japan but you need a forwarder
- Real cost per card (20-card batch): €23-25

**CGC Cards (Bulk Tier)**

- Nominal price: $15 (about €13.80)
- European office: no official one, but submission via UK partner (Vintage Cards UK)
- Total shipping for 20-card batch: €60-90
- Time: 30-45 days
- Real cost per card: €17-19

**Beckett (BGS Standard)**

- Nominal price: $30 (about €27.60)
- Shipping: same as PSA
- Time: 90-120 days
- Real cost per card: €35-40

## Sale premium: who pays more?

Data from 200 eBay sales (January 2025 - April 2026) of Pokemon cards, vintage and modern:

**Modern cards (SWSH and SV era)**

- PSA 10: +280% over raw NM
- CGC 10 Pristine: +210%
- CGC 10: +180%
- BGS 10 Black Label: +400%
- BGS 9.5: +120%

**Vintage cards (Base Set, Neo, EX era)**

- PSA 10: +450%
- CGC 10: +280%
- BGS 10: +380%

## What this means

If you sell modern cards, **PSA remains the standard**. The sale premium compensates the extra cost. CGC is a good alternative if you are in a hurry or have a limited budget.

For vintage, PSA is still king. The value difference between PSA 10 and CGC 10 on a Base Set Charizard can be €2,000+.

BGS Black Label is the holy grail but the achievement rate is under 1%. Do not chase it.

## When NOT to grade

- Raw value below €50: the math does not add up
- Card with visible defects at first glance: you risk PSA 7-8 which sell worse than raw
- You need liquidity within 60 days
- Modern card with massive print run (Charizard ex 151 base): supply of PSA 10s will always be high

## When you absolutely should grade

- Rare cards with perfect centering (check with ruler)
- Vintage cards in mint condition
- SIR/Alt Art of sets under 1 year old (capture premium before market saturates with graded copies)

## European workflow that works

1. Select only cards with raw value over €100
2. Verify centering, surface, edges with 4K photos
3. Submit to CGC via UK partner for cards under €300 (faster margin)
4. Submit to PSA for cards over €300 (sale premium justifies the cost)
5. List immediately post-grading on the richest market for that card (USA for vintage, EU for modern)

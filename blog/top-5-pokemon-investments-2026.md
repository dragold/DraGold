# Top 5 Pokemon cards to invest in for 2026

Investing in Pokemon cards is not gambling. It is analysis: scarcity, market trends, demand cycles. Based on FMV data from the last 18 months across English, Japanese and EU markets, here are the five cards with the best risk/reward profile for 2026.

## 1. Umbreon VMAX Alt Art (Evolving Skies, 215/203)

The undisputed queen of the Sword & Shield era. Since its 2021 release it has gained 380% raw and over 500% in PSA 10. The floor seems stable above €500 raw and over €1,500 in PSA 10.

Why it will keep climbing: Evolving Skies is one of the most opened sets, which paradoxically did not increase the supply of high-quality alt arts. Centered, gradeable copies are scarce.

Risk: price already high. Recommended entry below €480 raw. Japanese version is currently $720 vs $580 EN, so consider both markets.

## 2. Charizard ex Special Art Rare (151, 199/165)

The 151 set reignited first-generation nostalgia, and Charizard is always Charizard. The SAR saw an initial price dump but is consolidating around €140. Historically, Charizard SARs from every set reach their peak at least two years after release.

Why it is worth it: Charizard brand, evergreen set, possibility of limited reprints in 2026.

Risk: high stock in circulation. Only buy already-centered copies.

## 3. Lugia V Alt Art (Silver Tempest, 186/195)

Undervalued vs Umbreon VMAX despite the art being among the best of the SWSH era. It is up 85% in the last year, with wide margins still vs its "older sister".

Thesis: the market is realizing that after exhausting Umbreon, collectors move to the second most desired alt art of Silver Tempest.

Risk: depends on the Lugia narrative which remains strong but not as much as Eevee.

## 4. Pikachu with Grey Felt Hat (Van Gogh Museum Promo, SWSH285)

Promo from the Van Gogh Museum, pulled from circulation after resale controversies. Artificial scarcity + cross-collecting appeal (art + Pokemon).

Current floor: €280-340 raw. PSA 10 over €900. Already did a 4x since 2023 but demand remains stable.

Risk: counterfeits in circulation. Only buy from PSA-graded sellers or with museum receipt.

## 5. Miriam Special Illustration Rare (Scarlet & Violet base, 247/198)

Trainer SIR, still affordable price (around €90 raw). Trainer SIRs from the first sets of a new era have historically performed well once the era is closed.

Thesis: SV base era will run through end of 2026, after which prices of iconic SIRs will consolidate.

Risk: the set is not closed yet, possible reprints in special collections.

## Bottom line

Investing in Pokemon cards in 2026 means betting on real scarcity, not hype. Three rules that always apply:

- Verify centering with photos
- Only buy from sellers with high feedback
- Diversify across eras (no all-in on SV)

# Is pack opening profitable? Real numbers from 100 Surging Sparks boosters

The million-euro question: does opening Pokemon packs actually make economic sense, or are you just burning money for the thrill?

We opened 100 Surging Sparks packs (set released November 2024), tracked every single pull and recorded the FMV at the moment of opening. Here is what happened.

## Test setup

- Average pack retail price (Europe): €4.80
- Average pack online price (Cardmarket sealed): €4.20
- Total invested: €420
- Opening period: January 2026

## Raw results

Out of 100 packs:

- Ultra Rare hit rate or higher: 38%
- Special Illustration Rare (SIR) hit rate: 4%
- Hyper Rare hit rate: 2%
- Pikachu ex SAR (chase card): 1 copy
- Latias ex SAR: 1 copy

Total FMV of pulls: €487.

Gross profit: +€67 (+16%).

## But there is a big BUT

That +16% is not money in your pocket. To realize it you must:

- List each card individually
- Ship them (€1-3 per sale)
- Pay eBay commission (13%) or Cardmarket (5-10% combined)
- Wait 2-6 months to liquidate the stock

Subtracting average commissions and shipping, the real net margin drops to +3% / -5%. Practically break-even.

## When pack opening actually pays off

Three scenarios where the numbers tilt in your favor:

**1. Newly released set with strong hype**

In the first two weeks post-release, SIRs and hyper rares sell at 30-40% above their stabilized FMV. If you open on day 1, list on day 2, you have a real time arbitrage. Example: Pikachu ex SAR from Surging Sparks opened at €90, today is at €55.

**2. Bulk sold by weight**

Keep the good cards, sell common/uncommons in bulk at €0.02-0.05 each. 400 bulk cards = €12-20 extra that flips the margin.

**3. Vintage sealed product to hold**

Do not open. Buying booster boxes of vintage sets (Evolving Skies, Lost Origin) and keeping them sealed has returned +40-180% in 2-3 years.

## When NOT to open

- "Filler" sets between two important releases (e.g. many trainer challenge expansions)
- When the chase is a single card out of 200+ (math against you)
- When packs cost over €5.50 retail

## The uncomfortable truth

Opening packs is entertainment. If you enjoy it, fine: the realistic expected margin is zero. If you want to make money with Pokemon cards, buy singles of the chase cards once they have dropped 40% post-hype, and hold.
